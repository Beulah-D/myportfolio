02 · Profile

About

I'm a strategy and operations analyst with a Master's in Analytics from the University of Southern California.

At Triton Technical Services I led the rollout of the company's first operating system and redesigned how timesheets and invoices move through it. Before that, at LA General Medical Center, I was part of a four-person team that studied patient flow in interventional radiology and took its recommendations to hospital leadership.

My approach is the same each time. I talk to the people doing the work, map what happens today, find where time is lost, and back each recommendation with numbers. I use SQL, Python and Tableau for the analysis. Most of the job is getting people to agree on a better way of working and then making it stick.
