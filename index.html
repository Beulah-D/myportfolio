02 · About

I live where messy operations meet clean data.

I'm a strategy and operations analyst with a Master's in Analytics from the University of Southern California. My favorite problems start as undefined chaos. A tangle of manual spreadsheets, undocumented handoffs, or a process nobody has mapped. I turn them into systems that run without me.

I've done that inside a Level I trauma center's radiology division, on the floor of a technical-services firm building its ERP from zero, and across consumer-tech research. What stays constant is simple. I talk to the people doing the work, find where time leaks out, and back every recommendation with numbers.

I'm fluent across the tools that make that possible, from SQL and Tableau to process mapping and requirements gathering. But the real skill is translation: turning what stakeholders, operators, and leadership say into workflows and roadmaps they will actually adopt.
